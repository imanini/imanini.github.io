Introduction

My name is Isabella Manini, and I am a student at Windward Community College. This website was created as a part of my ICS 107 course. For my final project, I was tasked with creating a website that incorporates elements of HTML that I have learned about in this class. I was granted freedom to choose what I wanted my website to be about, and I chose to focus on student mental health. As a student who has their ups and downs with mental health, I thought it would be nice if students struggling with mental health got advice from another fellow student because I believe it's good for students to resonate and connect with other students about topics such as mental health. I feel that many mental health resources aren't specifically focused on students, and I also believe that most mental health resources are created by adults. I want to create a resource fully created by a student going through mental health to show students that they are not alone in this journey. As more and more students are exposed to and experiencing mental health, I believe that there is a bigger need for accessible mental health resources that students can rely on and use.

Purpose of This Website

This project focuses on raising awareness about student mental health. The goal is to create a space where students can find helpful information, simple coping strategies, and supportive resources. This site includes several different pages full of advice and resources for students to carry with them in their day-to-day lives.

  • Provide information about mental health
  • Share coping strategies for students
  • Offer access to helpful resources
