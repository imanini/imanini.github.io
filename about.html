Introduction

My name is Isabella Manini, and I am a student at Windward Community College. This website was created as part of my ICS 107 course.

I am currently learning about web development and HTML. Since I am still early in the course, this website will continue to develop and improve throughout the rest of the semester.

Purpose of This Website

This project focuses on raising awareness about student mental health. The goal is to create a space where students can find helpful information, simple coping strategies, and supportive resources.

  • Provide information about mental health
  • Share coping strategies for students
  • Offer access to helpful resources
