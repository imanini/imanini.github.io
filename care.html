Taking Care of Yourself

Self-care is important for maintaining mental wellbeing, especially during busy or stressful times. As a student, you are constantly balancing assignments, deadlines, extracurricular activities, and responsibilities outside of school. It can be easy to put your own needs aside in order to keep up with everything. However, ignoring your mental and physical health can make stress feel even more overwhelming over time. Taking small steps to care for yourself can help you feel more in control and better prepared to handle daily challenges.

Self-care does not have to be complicated or time-consuming. Many people think self-care means doing something big or expensive, but in reality, it often comes down to simple, consistent habits. Things like getting enough rest, eating properly, and taking short breaks throughout the day can make a noticeable difference. The goal of self-care is to create a routine that helps you feel more balanced, focused, and supported as you go through your day.

Simple Self-Care Ideas

  • Getting enough sleep each night
  • Eating regular and balanced meals
  • Spending time outdoors
  • Listening to music or doing relaxing activities
  • Taking breaks from screens

Why These Self-Care Tips Help

Tip #1

Sleep is essential to help your mind and body rest and recover from the adventures you had during the day. If you're not getting enough sleep, it makes you feel groggy, which makes it hard for you to focus throughout the day. For me, getting at least eight hours of sleep allows my mind to feel rested and prepared for the day ahead. If you struggle with falling asleep or staying asleep, then I'd suggest taking melatonin gummies or pills. Melatonin is a natural hormone that our brain produces that tells our bodies when to sleep, but not everyone's brain produces enough. Insomnia is a common sleep disorder that makes it hard to fall asleep and stay asleep. Melatonin gummies and pills help your brain to produce the hormone so that you can fall asleep sooner and stay asleep. As a student who has insomnia, I find that taking a melatonin supplement is helpful. However, I would consult with your doctor and discuss what dosage is appropriate for you to take if you consider getting melatonin gummies or pills.

Tip #2

Food is essential for fueling both your body and your brain. When you skip meals or rely on quick, unhealthy options, your energy levels can drop, making it harder to stay focused and productive. Instead of skipping meals or relying on sugary foods and energy drinks to stay awake, keep a supply of foods you like and eat small amounts of them throughout the day. However, I do understand that a common side effect for many mental conditions and disorders include loss of appetite. As someone who frequently lacks an appetite or desire to eat, I make sure that there are healthy foods at home that I actually like and I eat those foods. Some foods I keep at home include fruits, yogurt, bagels, rice, cereal, lettuce, salad ingredients, and sandwich ingredients. If you find it extremely difficult to be consistent with meals and specific foods, I would talk to a doctor about ways to overcome that inconsistency or ask about vitamin/mineral supplements. Even if it's hard for you to consistently eat, your body still deserves to get the vitamins and nutrients it needs.

Tip #3

Spending time outdoors, even for a short period, can have a positive impact on your mental health. Whether you go on a 20 minute walk or spend a day at the beach, it's rejuvenating to be in nature. Exposing yourself to fresh air and natural light can help reduce stress and boost your overall mood, especially after a long day at school. You spend enough time indoors during school, so take a break from being indoors and go on an adventure outside! I don't get out often, but I find it relaxing and refreshing when I take the time to go on a walk or go to the beach. You don't have to spend a lot of time outdoors, but I definitely believe that it's beneficial to go outside.

Tip #4

Taking time to do something you enjoy is an important part of self-care. Activities like listening to music, drawing, reading, or even just sitting quietly can help calm your mind and reduce stress. When you are constantly focused on schoolwork or responsibilities, your brain doesn’t get a chance to rest. Relaxing activities give you that break and help you recharge. Listening to music is a great thing to do when taking care of yourself. Music is a universal language, and each song tells a different story. It's a great feeling when you're listening to a song that resonates with you and relates to you. For me, music drowns out any outside thoughts I may have had and allows me to immerse myself in the lyrics and meaning og the song. This can apply to any activity. If it makes you feel calm and happy, then do it. You can use that happiness as a push to do better in your work.

Tip #5

Students spend a lot of time on screens, whether it’s for assignments, studying, or personal use. While technology is helpful, too much screen time can lead to mental fatigue, eye strain, and difficulty concentrating. Taking regular breaks from screens allows your eyes and mind to rest. I would suggest taking a break every 30-45 minutes. I usually find it hard to concentrate after spending that amount of time on a screen. When you step away from the screen, you can simply look outside a window or observe the objects around you to give your eyes a small break.
