Finding Support

Reaching out for support is an important step in taking care of your mental health. Many schools and communities offer services to help students.

These services are there to listen, guide, and provide help when needed.

Types of Support Available

  • School counselors
  • Student support centers
  • Peer support groups
  • Community mental health services
