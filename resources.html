Support Resources

If you are struggling or just need someone to talk to, these resources are available to support you. Reaching out can feel difficult, but there are trained professionals and organizations ready to listen and help.

Hotlines and Immediate Support

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    Call or text: 988
    Website: 988lifeline.org
  • Crisis Text Line
    Text: HOME to 741741
    Website: crisistextline.org
  • National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) Helpline
    Call: 1-800-950-NAMI (6264)
    Website: nami.org/help

Student & Counseling Support

  • School or College Counseling Services
    Most schools offer free counseling services for students. Check your campus website or student center for more information.
  • FindTreatment.gov
    Website: findtreatment.gov
    Helps you locate mental health professionals near you.
  • Psychology Today Therapist Finder
    Website: psychologytoday.com
    Allows you to search for therapists based on location and needs.

Final Note

You are not alone, and support is always available. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, please call 911 or your local emergency number. Reaching out is a strong and important step toward taking care of yourself.
