Managing Stress and Anxiety

Students often experience stress from school, responsibilities, and daily life. Learning simple coping strategies can make it easier to manage overwhelming emotions and stay focused.

These strategies are meant to be easy to use and can be practiced anywhere, whether at school or at home.

Examples of Coping Strategies

  • Deep breathing exercises
  • Taking short breaks while studying
  • Writing thoughts in a journal
  • Talking to a trusted friend
  • Practicing mindfulness or meditation
