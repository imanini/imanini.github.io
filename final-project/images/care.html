Taking Care of Yourself

Self-care is important for maintaining mental wellbeing, especially during busy or stressful times. Small actions can make a big difference in how you feel.

Self-care does not have to be complicated. It can be simple and fit into your daily routine.

Simple Self-Care Ideas

  • Getting enough sleep each night
  • Eating regular and balanced meals
  • Spending time outdoors
  • Listening to music or relaxing activities
  • Taking breaks from screens
