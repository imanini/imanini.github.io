Understanding Mental Health

Mental health affects how people think, feel, and act. It plays an important role in how students handle stress, relate to others, and make decisions.

Learning about mental health can help reduce stigma and make it easier to seek support when needed.

Common Challenges Students Face

  • Stress from schoolwork and exams
  • Anxiety in social or academic situations
  • Feeling overwhelmed or burned out
  • Difficulty balancing responsibilities
